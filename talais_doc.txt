1. Project Overview

TALAIS is a web-based Student Information System (SIS) developed specifically for Musuan Integrated School, a public elementary school located in Musuan, Maramag, Bukidnon. The school currently serves 34 teachers and 962 students across Kinder to Grade 6. At present, the school relies on DepEd's Learner Information System (LIS), which provides only minimal functionality, alongside manual and paper-based methods for the majority of its administrative and academic processes.



This document consolidates the comprehensive features list and detailed database schema for TALAIS, derived from the following primary sources:


Direct interviews with the school principal, Dr. Weenkie Jhon A. Marcelo, and faculty members (February 4, 2026)


Audio-recorded interview transcript with school staff discussing enrollment, grading, attendance, and sectioning processes


MIS Interview notes documenting digitalization requirements


The TALAIS capstone manuscript (Chapter I–IV)


ITSD 82 Mini Project Criteria Guide (BSIT 3C)



The system is designed to address the following core problems identified at Musuan Integrated School:


Enrollment is still entirely manual, requiring parents to fill out physical forms and submit birth certificates during Brigada Eskwela.


Grading and form generation (Form 137, Form 138 / SF9, SF10) are done manually by hand, making retrieval and accuracy difficult.


Attendance (SF2) is recorded manually in paper form without digital integration.


Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and NAT performance data are computed by hand every year, risking data loss.


Student health data (BMI for feeding program) is tracked in Excel by teachers with no integrated system.


Scheduling and class section assignment is coordinated informally among grade level chairs.


There is no centralized platform for parents to monitor their child's grades, attendance, or school violations.


Alumni and transfer student tracing is difficult; physical records deteriorate over time.




2. System User Roles

TALAIS implements Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with three distinct user types, each with defined access scopes derived from the school's actual organizational structure. Student accounts are not included — as Musuan Integrated School is an elementary school, students are not expected to directly interact with the system. Student data is instead managed and monitored by their teachers, administrators, and parents/guardians.

3.1 Enrollment Module

Based on the audio interview and follow-up confirmation, enrollment at Musuan Integrated School takes place during Brigada Eskwela. The school arranges tables by grade level — from Kinder to Grade 6 — with an assigned adviser at each table. Parents approach the table for their child's target grade level and fill out the physical enrollment form there. Section assignment does NOT happen during this initial enrollment; all students are first registered under a grade level, and sections are divided afterward (once the qualifying exam results are available for cream class placement). Continuing students have existing LRNs from Kinder onward. The enrollment module digitizes this flow while preserving the grade-level-first, sectioning-later workflow.
